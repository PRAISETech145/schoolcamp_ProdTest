Genetics & DNA
DNA Structure & Replication Basic
DNA: Double helix; sugar-phosphate backbone; bases inside
Base pairs: A=T (2 H-bonds) | G≡C (3 H-bonds)
Nucleotide = phosphate + deoxyribose sugar + nitrogenous base
Replication: Semi-conservative; DNA polymerase + helicase
Gene: section of DNA coding for a protein/polypeptide
Genome: complete set of genetic material in an organism
Protein Synthesis Intermediate
Transcription (nucleus):
DNA --> mRNA (RNA polymerase; template strand read 3' to 5')
T in DNA --> A in mRNA | A --> U | G --> C | C --> G

Translation (ribosome):
mRNA codon (3 bases) --> tRNA anticodon --> amino acid
Start codon: AUG (methionine)
Stop codons: UAA, UAG, UGA
Mendel's Laws Basic
Law of Segregation: alleles separate during gamete formation
Law of Independent Assortment: genes on different chromosomes assort independently
Dominant (A): expressed when 1 or 2 copies present
Recessive (a): only expressed when 2 copies present (aa)
Genotype: genetic makeup (AA, Aa, aa)
Phenotype: physical expression of genotype

Important Cycles in Biology
The Carbon Cycle Basic
Carbon enters atmosphere via: respiration, combustion, decomposition, volcanic eruption
Carbon removed from atmosphere via: photosynthesis, dissolved in oceans
Photosynthesis: 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂
Respiration: C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + ATP
The Nitrogen Cycle Intermediate
Nitrogen fixation: N₂ → NH₃ (Rhizobium in root nodules; lightning)
Nitrification: NH₃ → NO₂⁻ → NO₃⁻ (Nitrosomonas → Nitrobacter)
Denitrification: NO₃⁻ → N₂ (anaerobic bacteria, e.g. Pseudomonas)
Ammonification: Organic N → NH₃ (decomposers/bacteria)
Assimilation: Plants absorb NO₃⁻ → amino acids → proteins
The Water Cycle Basic
Evaporation: water → water vapour (heat energy from sun)
Condensation: water vapour cools → liquid water droplets → clouds
Precipitation: rain, snow, sleet, hail fall to Earth
Transpiration: plants release water vapour through stomata
Surface run-off: water flows over land into rivers/oceans
Infiltration: water soaks into soil → groundwater
Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle) Advanced
Location: Matrix of mitochondria
Input per turn: 1 Acetyl-CoA (2C) + Oxaloacetate (4C)
Output per turn: 2 CO₂ + 3 NADH + 1 FADH₂ + 1 ATP (GTP)
Per glucose (x2 turns): 4 CO₂ + 6 NADH + 2 FADH₂ + 2 ATP
Oxaloacetate: regenerated each turn to accept next Acetyl-CoA
The Cell Cycle Intermediate
G₁ (Gap 1): cell grows; produces proteins and organelles
S (Synthesis): DNA replication — genome duplicated
G₂ (Gap 2): further growth; prepares for division; checks DNA
M (Mitotic): nuclear division (mitosis) + cytokinesis
G₀: cells exit cycle; resting or specialised

The Cardiac Cycle Intermediate
Diastole: heart muscle relaxes; chambers fill with blood
Atrial systole: atria contract → push blood into ventricles
Ventricular systole: ventricles contract → blood pumped to lungs (right) and body (left)
Heart rate: ~72 beats/min at rest
Cardiac output: CO = heart rate × stroke volume [L/min]
Blood pressure: systolic / diastolic (normal ≈ 120/80 mmHg)
The Calvin Cycle (Light-Independent Reactions) Advanced
Location: Stroma of chloroplast
Step 1 — Carbon fixation: CO₂ + RuBP (5C) → 2 × 3-PGA (3C) [RuBisCO enzyme]
Step 2 — Reduction: 3-PGA + ATP + NADPH → G3P
Step 3 — Regeneration: 5 G3P + ATP → 3 RuBP (cycle continues)
Net output per 3 CO₂: 1 G3P (used for glucose synthesis)
For 1 glucose (6C): 6 CO₂ + 18 ATP + 12 NADPH
Ecology
Energy Flow & Pyramids Basic
Food chain: Producer → Primary consumer → Secondary → Tertiary
Trophic levels: L1=Producers | L2=Herbivores | L3=Carnivores | L4=Top carnivores
10% rule: only ~10% of energy passed to next trophic level
Energy lost as: heat, excretion, respiration, uneaten biomass
Pyramid of biomass: total dry mass at each trophic level
Pyramid of numbers: number of organisms at each level
Population Ecology Intermediate
Exponential growth: dN/dt = rN (unlimited resources)
Logistic growth: dN/dt = rN(1 - N/K)
K = carrying capacity
r = intrinsic rate of natural increase
Biotic factors: predation, competition, disease, food supply
Abiotic factors: temperature, light, water, pH, salinity

Plant Biology
Photosynthesis Basic
Overall equation:
6CO₂ + 6H₂O → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ (light + chlorophyll)

Light-dependent (thylakoid membrane):
H₂O + light → ATP + NADPH + O₂ (photolysis of water)

Light-independent / Calvin cycle (stroma):
CO₂ + ATP + NADPH → G3P → Glucose

Factors limiting photosynthesis: light intensity, CO₂ concentration, temperature.

Aerobic & Anaerobic Respiration Basic
Aerobic:
C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6O₂ → 6CO₂ + 6H₂O + 38 ATP

Anaerobic (animals/yeast):
C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2 lactic acid + 2 ATP (animals)
C₆H₁₂O₆ → 2 ethanol + 2CO₂ + 2 ATP (yeast)

Glycolysis (cytoplasm): Glucose → 2 Pyruvate + 2 ATP + 2 NADH
Link reaction (matrix): Pyruvate → Acetyl-CoA + CO₂ + NADH
Krebs cycle (matrix): 2 ATP + 6 NADH + 2 FADH₂ per glucose
Oxidative phosphorylation (inner membrane): ~34 ATP
Transpiration & Tropisms Basic
Transpiration: loss of water vapour through stomata
TACT mechanism: Transpiration, Adhesion, Cohesion, Tension
Factors increasing transpiration: high temp, low humidity, wind, light

Phototropism: growth towards light (IAA migrates away from light)
Gravitropism: roots grow down; shoots grow up
Hydrotropism: roots grow towards water
Thigmotropism: response to touch (tendrils coil)
Human Body Systems
Overview of All Body Systems Basic
Digestive System
  • Mouth → oesophagus → stomach → small intestine → large intestine → rectum
  • Enzymes: amylase (starch), protease (protein), lipase (fat)
  • Absorption: villi in small intestine
Circulatory System
  • Double circulation: pulmonary + systemic
  • Heart: 4 chambers (RA, LA, RV, LV)
  • Blood: plasma, RBC, WBC, platelets
  • CO = HR × SV
Respiratory System
  • Nose → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli
  • Gas exchange: O₂ in, CO₂ out across alveoli
  • Tidal volume ~500 mL; VC ~4.8 L
Nervous System
  • CNS: brain + spinal cord
  • PNS: sensory + motor nerves
  • Reflex arc: receptor → sensory → relay → motor → effector
Excretory System
  • Kidneys: filter blood → urine
  • Filtration → reabsorption → secretion
  • ADH controls water reabsorption
Immune System
  • Non-specific: skin, phagocytes, inflammation
  • Specific: B-cells (antibodies), T-cells (cell-mediated)
  • Memory cells → immunity
Reproductive System
  • Male: testes → sperm (spermatogenesis)
  • Female: ovaries → eggs (oogenesis)
  • Fertilisation → zygote → embryo
Endocrine System
  • Hormones: chemical messengers in blood
  • Insulin/glucagon: blood glucose control
  • ADH, oestrogen, testosterone, adrenaline
Biochemical Tests for Food Molecules
Tests for Biological Molecules
Reducing SugarsBasic
Benedict's Test
Add Benedict's reagent, heat in water bath — blue → orange/brick-red precipitate +ve
Reducing sugar + Cu2+(blue) → Cu2O↓(red) + oxidised sugar
Examples
Glucose, maltose, lactose, fructose (all monosaccharides)
Non-Reducing SugarsBasic
Sucrose Test
1. Benedict's → blue (negative) -ve
2. Hydrolyse with HCl + heat, neutralise with NaHCO₃
3. Benedict's → orange/red precipitate +ve after hydrolysis
Sucrose + H2O → glucose + fructose (then Benedict's +ve)
StarchBasic
Iodine Test
Add iodine solution (I₂/KI) — orange-brown → dark blue/black +ve
Blue-black colour due to iodine trapped in amylose helix
I₂ + amylose helix → blue-black complex
ProteinsBasic
Biuret Test
Add NaOH(aq) + dilute CuSO₄ — blue → purple/violet +ve
Tests for peptide bonds (-CO-NH-)
Cu2+ + peptide bonds → purple complex (biuret reaction)
Ninhydrin Test
Add ninhydrin + heat — colourless → purple/blue (amino acids) +ve
Lipids / FatsBasic
Emulsion Test (Sudan III)
Dissolve in ethanol, add water — white milky emulsion forms +ve
Or: add Sudan III/IV stain → red/orange layer
Fat + ethanol → solution; + H₂O → milky emulsion
Translucent spot test
Rub food on paper — translucent/greasy spot remains +ve
Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)Intermediate
DCPIP Test
Add DCPIP (blue dye) — blue → colourless (decolourises) +ve
More vitamin C → fewer drops needed to decolourise
DCPIP (blue) + ascorbic acid → DCPIP-H₂ (colourless)
Summary of Positive Results:
Reducing sugar (Benedict's): blue → orange/brick-red ppt
Non-reducing sugar (after hydrolysis): blue → orange/brick-red ppt
Starch (Iodine): orange-brown → dark blue/black
Protein (Biuret): blue → purple/violet
Lipid (Emulsion): clear → white milky emulsion
Vitamin C (DCPIP): blue → colourless
Evolution & Classification
Natural Selection (Darwin) Basic
Key principles:
1. Variation exists within a population
2. Offspring compete for limited resources (struggle for existence)
3. Better-adapted individuals survive and reproduce (survival of the fittest)
4. Favourable traits are inherited by offspring
5. Over many generations → evolution of new species (speciation)
Taxonomy & Classification Basic
Hierarchy (Domain → Kingdom → ...Species):
Domain → Kingdom → Phylum → Class → Order → Family → Genus → Species
Mnemonic: Dear King Philip Came Over For Good Soup
Binomial nomenclature: Genus species (e.g. Homo sapiens)
5 Kingdoms: Monera | Protista | Fungi | Plantae | Animalia

Immune Response & Vaccines Intermediate
Non-specific (innate):
Skin barrier → phagocytosis (neutrophils/macrophages) → inflammation → fever

Specific (adaptive):
B lymphocytes: produce antibodies (humoral immunity)
T lymphocytes: kill infected cells (cell-mediated immunity)
Memory cells: rapid response on second exposure

Vaccination: introduce antigen → primary immune response → memory cells formed
Herd immunity: when enough population vaccinated to protect whole community
