This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies SchoolCamp uses, why we use them, and how you can manage them.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember information about your visit, making your next visit easier and the site more useful to you.

localStorage and sessionStorage are similar browser technologies that store data locally on your device. localStorage persists until manually cleared; sessionStorage is cleared when you close the browser tab.

2 Cookies We Use

SchoolCamp uses only essential cookies and browser storage. Here is the complete list:

Name Type Purpose Duration
sessionid Cookie Keeps you logged in to SchoolCamp (Django session management) Session
csrftoken Cookie Protects all forms against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks 1 year
sc-theme localStorage Remembers whether you prefer dark mode or light mode Permanent*
sc_loaded sessionStorage Remembers if the page loader animation has already played this session Session

* Permanent means it persists until you clear your browser data or uninstall the browser.

3 What We Do NOT Use
🚫

SchoolCamp does not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, Google Analytics, third-party ad networks, fingerprinting, or any cross-site tracking technology. Your browsing on SchoolCamp stays on SchoolCamp.

4 Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings:

🦊
Firefox Menu → Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data
🌐
Chrome Menu → Settings → Privacy and Security → Clear browsing data
🔵
Microsoft Edge Menu → Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data
⚠️
Note Deleting cookies will log you out of SchoolCamp and reset your dark/light mode preference.
5 Your Consent

By using SchoolCamp, you consent to our use of the essential cookies and browser storage described in this policy. Since we only use strictly necessary cookies, no cookie consent banner is required under most privacy regulations.

If we ever add non-essential cookies in the future, we will update this policy and ask for your explicit consent.

6 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. We will notify you of significant changes via an in-app notice.
