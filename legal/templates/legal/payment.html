This policy explains how payments are handled on SchoolCamp, what your Premium subscription includes, and our policy on refunds.

1 Subscription Plans
🆓 Free Plan
0 XAF /month
  • Forum access
  • Group membership
  • Basic chat
  • Quiz participation
⭐ Premium Plan
500 XAF /month
  • Unlimited materials
  • Voice messages
  • Advanced quiz modes
  • Priority support
2 Payment Methods

Payments are processed exclusively through MTN Mobile Money (MoMo) in Central African Francs (XAF). We do not currently accept credit/debit cards, Orange Money, or other payment methods.

By initiating a payment, you authorise SchoolCamp to charge 500 XAF to your MTN MoMo account for each billing period.

3 Billing & Renewal

Your Premium subscription is billed monthly and will automatically renew on the same date each month unless you cancel before the renewal date.

  • You will receive a notification before your subscription renews.
  • If payment fails (e.g. insufficient MoMo balance), your account reverts to the Free Plan.
  • You can view your subscription status and renewal date under My Subscription.
4 Cancellation

You may cancel your Premium subscription at any time from your account settings under My Subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period — you will retain Premium access until that date.

Cancellation does not automatically trigger a refund for the current period.

5 Refund Policy

General rule: Due to the digital nature of our services, all subscription payments are generally non-refundable.

However, we may issue a refund in the following exceptional circumstances:

  • You were charged twice for the same billing period (billing error).
  • You did not receive access to Premium features after a successful payment.
  • The platform was completely unavailable for more than 72 consecutive hours during your paid period.

Refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the charge to support@schoolcamp.cm, with your MTN MoMo transaction reference.

6 Price Changes

SchoolCamp reserves the right to change subscription pricing. We will provide at least 30 days notice of any price increase via email and an in-app notification. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the new price.

7 Free Trial Terms

If you are offered a free trial:

  • The trial period and its duration will be clearly stated at sign-up.
  • No payment is required during the trial.
  • At the end of the trial, your account reverts to the Free Plan unless you subscribe.
  • Each user is eligible for one free trial only.
